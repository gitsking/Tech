# How to Replace Oil Filter
Before you proceed make sure the vehicle is stopped on a flat surface and engine is turned off.

## Prerequisites
- Wait for engine to cool-down before you start to work
- Keep the recommended engine oil **(SAE 15W-40 )** available for topping up or refilling after filter replacement.
- Wear appropriate PPE, including safety gloves and safety footwear.

## Tools Required
- Drain pan
- Oil filter wrench
- Funnel
  
## Procedure
1. Position the jack at the designated lifting point marked on the chassis. Raise the vehicle to a safe working height.

2. Position the drain pan beneath the oil filter.

3. Loosen the old oil filter using a filter wrench.

4. Unscrew the filter by hand and remove it carefully.

5. Clean the filter mounting surface with a clean cloth.

6. Apply a thin film of clean engine oil to the rubber sealing gasket of the new filter.

> **Note:** Do not install the new filter over an old gasket.

7. Install the new filter by hand until the gasket contacts the mounting surface.

> **Note:** Tighten the filter by hand. If required use wrench for tightening.Do not exceed 25 Nm of torque when installing the filter.

8. Lower and remove the jack.

**Warning:** Dispose of the used filter according to local regulations.

## Post Procedure:

1. Start the engine and let it run for 15 min.
  
2. Check underneath the vehicle for oil leaks.

3. Switch off the engine, wait for 15 min.
4.  recheck the engine oil level.

5. Reset the vehicle’s oil-service reminder.

6. Record the date, mileage, oil specification, and filter used.


**Result:** The Old filter is replaced and no oil leaks are observed
